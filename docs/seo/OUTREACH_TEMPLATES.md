# Outreach templates

US-158. Three templates, written to be sendable rather than to be adapted.
Targets are in `docs/seo/LINK_ACQUISITION.md`.

**Read the warning at the end of that file before sending any of these.** Four
things in this batch are still outstanding, and a reviewer will find all of
them.

## Rules these follow, and why

- **No fabricated social proof.** No customer counts, no "trusted by", no
  ratings, no revenue. The site has already had five invented testimonials and a
  fake 4.8-star rating removed. A journalist who checks one claim and finds it
  hollow will not check the second one — they will just not write about you.
- **Name the article.** A pitch that could have been sent to anybody gets
  treated as if it was.
- **Say who you are worse than.** It is the fastest way to be believed, and the
  comparison pages already say it in public.
- **One link.** `/press` answers the follow-up questions, so the email does not
  have to.
- **No follow-up sequence.** One reminder, once, then leave it. These are people,
  not a funnel.

Replace anything in `[square brackets]`. If you cannot fill one in honestly,
delete the sentence rather than softening it.

## 1. Roundup author

For the Tier 1 publications. Send to the byline on the specific article.

> **Subject:** Addition for your [ARTICLE TITLE] piece — link-in-bio built for agents
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> I read [ARTICLE TITLE] — the [SPECIFIC SECTION OR TOOL] section is the one I
> send people to when they ask about [TOPIC].
>
> One category it does not cover yet: link-in-bio pages built specifically for
> real estate. Agents use Linktree and similar, but those tools organise links;
> they cannot hold a listing with photos and a price, a buyer or seller enquiry
> form, or a showing booking. That gap is what we built AgentBio for.
>
> Honestly: for anyone who is not selling property, a general tool is the better
> choice, and our comparison page says so. This is a narrow product for a narrow
> audience.
>
> Everything you would need — what it does, what it costs, logos — is at
> https://agentbio.net/press. Happy to set up an account with real listings in
> it if you would rather click around than read about it.
>
> Either way, thanks for the piece.
>
> [YOUR NAME]
> [ROLE], AgentBio

**Before sending:** open the article, find something in it you actually used, and
put that in the second line. If nothing in it is useful to you, this is the
wrong article to pitch.

## 2. Directory or review platform submission

For the "about" field on G2, Capterra, GetApp, and similar. Not an email.

> **Short description (under 160 characters)**
>
> A link-in-bio page for real estate agents. Show your listings, capture buyer
> and seller enquiries, and take showing bookings from one link.
>
> **Full description**
>
> AgentBio is a link-in-bio page built for real estate agents. The single link
> an agent can put in an Instagram or Facebook profile leads to their active and
> sold listings with photos, prices and details; to buyer, seller and
> home-valuation forms that record where each enquiry came from; and to a
> calendar for booking showings and consultations.
>
> It is not a CRM, an MLS, or a website builder. For anyone outside real estate,
> a general link-in-bio tool will do the job better and usually cheaper.
>
> There is a free plan. Paid plans start at [STARTING PRICE — check
> https://agentbio.net/pricing] per month.
>
> **Category:** Link in Bio / Social Media Management
> **Audience:** Real estate agents, small teams, leasing and property management
> **Website:** https://agentbio.net
> **Press and product facts:** https://agentbio.net/press

**Before submitting:** check the price against `/pricing`. Do not type a number
from memory — that is exactly how `$39` ended up in six places on this site
describing a plan that does not exist.

## 3. Partnership — brokerage, team, or association

The highest-value and slowest of the three. This is a conversation opener, not a
pitch.

> **Subject:** Would this be useful to your agents?
>
> Hi [NAME],
>
> I build AgentBio — a link-in-bio page for real estate agents. Instead of a list
> of links, it holds the agent's listings with photos and prices, their enquiry
> forms, and a booking calendar, so the one link in an Instagram profile does
> something.
>
> I am not trying to sell you [N] seats. I would like to know whether it is
> actually useful to [BROKERAGE / ASSOCIATION] agents, and the fastest way to
> find out is for two or three of them to use it for a month and tell me where
> it falls short.
>
> If that is worth twenty minutes, I will set them up myself and stay out of
> the way. If it is not a fit, telling me why would genuinely help.
>
> https://agentbio.net/press has the details.
>
> [YOUR NAME]
> [ROLE], AgentBio

**Before sending:** be able to answer "what happens to the agent's page if they
leave the brokerage" and "who owns the leads". If you cannot, work that out
first — it is the second question every brokerage asks.

## What none of these do

They do not offer payment, affiliate commission, or a link exchange for a
placement. A paid link that is not marked as paid is a Google policy violation
and worth less than no link. If a site offers to list you for a fee, that is
advertising: it can be a fine thing to buy, but it should carry `rel="sponsored"`
and it is not what this document is about.

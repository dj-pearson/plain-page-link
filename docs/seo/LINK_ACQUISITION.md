# Link acquisition: targets, assets, and who does what

US-158. Researched 2026-09-08. **The outreach itself has not happened and is not
a code task** — see "Which half is human" at the end.

## Why this exists

Across the 16 months to 2026-09-08 the site averaged position **18.39 on desktop
and 20.40 on mobile**, and the average barely moved by page or by topic. Uniform
mid-page-two ranking irrespective of content quality is the signature of a
domain with almost no inbound links.

US-147 through US-157 fixed what was fixable in the code: the pages render, the
blog has bodies, the sitemap is real, the claims are true. That work moves pages
from roughly position 20 to roughly position 10. **It cannot move them to 1-5.**
What does is being cited by pages that already rank.

**A competitor will not add you to their roundup.** Pitching them wastes the
effort and tells them you exist. The lesson is that this SERP is won by
*publishing a better page than theirs* (US-155 and US-156 are the start of that),
not by outreach to it.

Real link targets are one step out: the industry publications agents actually
read, the review platforms buyers check, and the communities agents are in.

Effort/likelihood, honestly: HousingWire and The Close update these pieces on a
cycle and do respond to well-made pitches, but they review what they list — a
half-finished product gets a paragraph you would rather not have. **Do this
after the outstanding work below is done, not before.**

## Tier 2 — review platforms

Buyers of SaaS check these, and they rank for "<product> reviews" and
"<competitor> alternatives" queries. Listing is self-serve.

- **G2** — vendor profile is free to claim; category is likely "Link in Bio" or
  "Social Media Management".
- **Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice** (all Gartner) — one submission covers
  several properties.
- **Product Hunt** — a launch is a one-day event with a lasting link. Worth
  doing once the product is genuinely ready to be looked at.

**These require real reviews from real customers to be worth anything.** Do not
solicit reviews you would not be comfortable seeing quoted, and do not write
them. The site has already had five fabricated testimonials and an invented
4.8-star rating removed (US-156, US-157).

## Tier 3 — where agents actually are

Lower authority, higher relevance, and much harder to fake:

- **r/realtors** and **r/RealEstate** on Reddit — both ban self-promotion; the
  only honest route is being useful for months first, or answering a question
  where the product is genuinely the answer.
- **Facebook groups for agents** — same rule, usually enforced by one admin.
- **Brokerage intranets and vendor lists** — a partnership conversation, not an
  outreach email. One brokerage adopting the product is worth more than any
  roundup on this page.
- **Local and state Realtor associations** — many run member-benefit vendor
  programmes.

## What was NOT verified

Every URL in Tier 1 was retrieved on 2026-09-08 and is real. **The Tier 2 and
Tier 3 entries are categories, not verified submission links** — I did not open
G2's vendor signup, Capterra's listing form, or any subreddit's current rules,
so do not treat those as checked. Confirm each before working from it.

No contact names or email addresses are listed here, because I did not verify
any and a wrong name is worse than no name. Find the author on the byline of the
specific article you are pitching.

## The on-site assets

Built in this story, at a stable URL a pitch can point at:

- **`/press`** — what the product is, what it does, what it costs, who it is
  for, and logo downloads. Every figure comes from `PRICING_PLANS`, so the page
  cannot drift from what the checkout charges. It carries no customer counts,
  funding, awards, press mentions or ratings, because none of those are things
  we can substantiate.
- Logos already existed and are now linked from one place: `/logo.png`,
  `/logo.webp`, `/Icon.png`, `/Icon.webp`, `/Cover.png`, `/Cover.webp`.
- **Product screenshots are deliberately absent.** Real ones need a populated
  account and a person to choose what to show; generating something
  representative would mean inventing an agent's listings. The page says to ask
  rather than showing stale or invented images.

## Which half is human

Written down because an agent can produce the artefacts and cannot do the work.

**Done, in code:**
- `/press` with accurate, self-updating product facts
- Logo files linked from one place
- Outreach templates — `docs/seo/OUTREACH_TEMPLATES.md`
- This target list

**Not done, and not doable here — a person must:**
1. Decide whether the product is ready to be reviewed. Tier 1 publications test
   what they list. See "Before you send anything" below.
2. Find the current author of each Tier 1 article and send the pitch.
3. Claim the G2 and Capterra profiles and complete them.
4. Ask real customers for reviews, and accept whatever they write.
5. Have the partnership conversations. No email substitutes for these.

**Nothing in this story contacted anyone, created an account, submitted a
listing, or claims any placement exists.**

### Before you send anything

of them:

  `docs/seo/BLOG_CONSOLIDATION.md` (US-154).
- **Seven fabricated testimonials are still live** on the tools pages,
  `/instagram-bio-for-realtors` and `/features/analytics` — listed at the end of
  the US-156 entry in `progress.txt`.
- **The articles snapshot and the Cloudflare deploy hook** are unconfigured, so
  new posts do not reach the index — `docs/deploy/BLOG_PRERENDERING.md` (US-148).
- **No city page is indexable**, by design, until one has real local content —
  `docs/seo/CITY_PAGES.md` (US-153).

Pitching HousingWire a product whose own site quotes agents who may not exist is
a worse outcome than not pitching at all.
